# Sim to Real

Teleoperate a simulated reBot arm in Isaac Sim, record datasets there, train a policy on them, then run it on the real arm.

## Links

- [rebot-arm-dli-isaacsim](https://github.com/Seeed-Projects/rebot-arm-dli-isaacsim): Seeed's Isaac Sim project for this arm. See the Sim to Real tutorial step.
- [Train an SO-101 Robot From Sim-to-Real With NVIDIA Isaac](https://docs.nvidia.com/learning/physical-ai/sim-to-real-so-101/latest/): NVIDIA's free course, with its [workshop code](https://github.com/isaac-sim/Sim-to-Real-SO-101-Workshop).
- [LeRobot: ACT](https://huggingface.co/docs/lerobot/act): training and running the policy on the real arm.
- [ACT paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.13705) and [domain randomization paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1703.06907).
