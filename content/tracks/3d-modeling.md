# 3D Modeling

Move a wrist camera around an object, reconstruct it, and pick each next viewpoint by what is still unseen instead of following a fixed orbit. The arm's kinematics give the camera pose for every frame.

## Links

- [Nerfstudio](https://docs.nerf.studio/): reconstruction from posed images, NeRF or Gaussian splats.
- [COLMAP](https://colmap.github.io/): classic photogrammetry.
- [Open3D reconstruction system](https://www.open3d.org/docs/latest/tutorial/reconstruction_system/index.html): fusing RealSense depth frames.
- [Hand-eye calibration with OpenCV](https://roboflow.com/blog/hand-eye-calibration): the camera-to-gripper transform.
- [nbvplanner](https://github.com/ethz-asl/nbvplanner): a reference next-best-view planner, and the papers [ActiveNeRF](https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.08546) and [ActiveSplat](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.21955).
